Infrastructure & responsibility

Built around control, clarity and discretion.

Financial technology for organisations that take operational accountability seriously. Privacy is an important design objective, but it should never be confused with an unverified security claim.

◈

Connected to the Lunul network

TermiNull is configured for the Lunul network, chain ID 448000, with USDT as the stated settlement asset. Supported balances and transaction details depend on the deployed contracts and connected wallet.

⌘

Designed for accountable operations

Connected-wallet access, transaction confirmation and supported record exports help teams review activity. Organisations should retain appropriate internal controls, authorisation procedures, accounting records and independent compliance processes.

◇

Privacy with transparent status

Shielding and unshielding are not currently available while the required pool, verifier and proof interfaces remain pending. No active ZK privacy or anonymous transaction capability is implied.

▧

Reporting grounded in records

Transaction reports should distinguish confirmed network activity from pending requests and internal database records. On-chain confirmation alone is not a substitute for a complete accounting audit.
